Privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information is involved when you play Retro Invaders, a game made by retro123321, on Android, iOS or PC. If you have any questions, email ac261996@gmail.com.

What the game keeps on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following on your own device:

  • your settings, such as volume, controls, screen shake and difficulty
  • your best kills and coins for each difficulty
  • your coin total and which ships you have unlocked

This information stays on your device. It is never sent to us, and you don’t need an account to play. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

What we collect

We don’t collect, store or receive personal information about you. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of our own. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Advertising on phones and tablets

The Android and iOS versions of Retro Invaders show ads, provided by Google AdMob. Ads appear after some games end, and you can choose to watch one to continue a game. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use information such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID
  • your IP address, and an approximate location worked out from it
  • information about your device and the app, such as the model, operating system and language
  • how you interact with ads

Google may use this information to show personalized ads where the law allows and you have agreed. Learn how Google uses this information in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings, then Privacy options.
  • On any phone or tablet, you can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings. On Android, look under Settings, then Google, then Ads, or Settings, then Privacy, then Ads. On iPhone and iPad, look under Settings, then Privacy & Security.

The PC version

The PC version has no ads, and the game itself collects no information. The store you buy it from, such as Steam, has its own privacy policy.

Children

Retro Invaders is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: ac261996@gmail.com
